forge set, and inherited anti-goals. Move parent task to `promoted/`.

6. Verify structural artifacts: child manifest with `parent_id`, parent
   task moved to `docket/promoted/` with `promoted_to_child_eidos`

   `cardinality=pair-or-pod-required`.
